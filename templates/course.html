The Program

Course Overview

A structured path from the foundations of English toward confident, natural communication.

LEVEL 01

A1

Build the fundamental vocabulary and structure needed to begin communicating.

  • Core vocabulary
  • Basic sentence structure
  • Essential verbs
  • Basic questions
  • Everyday communication
Start Module: Through vs. Across
LEVEL 02

A2

Expand vocabulary and begin expressing more detailed ideas and experiences.

  • Expanded vocabulary
  • Past and future structures
  • Descriptions
  • Common expressions
  • Longer sentences
LEVEL 03

B1

Develop independence and communicate ideas with greater flexibility.

  • Complex sentences
  • All major tense systems
  • Connectors
  • Expanded vocabulary
  • Conversation skills
LEVEL 04

B2

Develop precision, fluency, natural expressions, and sophisticated communication.

  • Advanced vocabulary
  • Nuance and precision
  • Idiomatic language
  • Natural conversation
  • Advanced grammar

How the course works

Each stage builds on previously introduced vocabulary and structures. Lessons combine grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, comprehension, and practical use so that students learn English as a connected system rather than as isolated rules.
